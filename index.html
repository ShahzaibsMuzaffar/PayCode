Help & Walkthrough

Disclaimer

This app only opens your phone dialer with a code. It cannot read the reply, move money, or confirm a payment. It works only with SIMs whose operator supports UPI over USSD (*99# works on many operators such as Airtel, not all). Other SIMs or countries may use different codes; change them in Settings. Receipts are sender-reported, not bank-verified. Always check your bank SMS.

Before setup (one time)
  1. Use the mobile number linked to your bank account. The SIM must be in this phone.
  2. Dial *99# and choose your language.
  3. Choose Profile and register: select your bank, then enter the last 6 digits and expiry of your debit card.
  4. Set your UPI PIN when asked. Remember it and never share it.
  5. Wait for the confirmation message, then test with ₹1 to ₹10.
How to send money
  1. Scan the receiver's QR code, or open Send to UPI ID.
  2. Enter the amount in the app first.
  3. Tap Continue to Dialer, then press call.
  4. Paste the copied ID when asked, enter the same amount, then your PIN.
  5. Return to the app and mark Success or Failed. Add the reference number.
  6. Share the receipt image with the receiver.
Check balance
  1. Tap Check Balance on the home screen, press call, and enter your UPI PIN.
Troubleshooting
  • Dialer does not open: use Chrome and allow it to open the phone app.
  • Code shows invalid: your SIM uses another code. Edit it in Settings.
  • Limits apply, typically ₹5,000 per payment on *99#.
  • Debited but not received: contact your bank with the reference number.
  • Data stays on this device. Export CSV for a backup.

Settings

Defaults are the standard *99# UPI codes. Check your operator's codes. Placeholders {upi}, {mobile}, {amount} are supported for one-line codes.

App lock

This PIN only locks Paycode on this device. Never use your UPI PIN here. If you forget it, clear this site's data (history will be deleted).

About

About the app

Paycode v1.0

Paycode helps you make UPI payments without mobile data. It reads the receiver's QR code, copies the UPI ID, opens your dialer with the USSD code, and keeps a clear record of every payment you mark.

Your history lives only on this device. There are no accounts, no tracking, and nothing is ever sent anywhere. Paycode never sees your UPI PIN.
